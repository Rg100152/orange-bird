About Orange Bird

A small social network for big imagined worlds.

Orange Bird is a demonstration social site for AI artists, built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It borrows the flat, three-column feel of the early 2010s web and fills it with 61 artworks from five fictional creators.

Create. Share. Fly.

The nest, explained.

No feed algorithm, no growth hacks, no infinite casino. Just a timeline, a gallery and a few good prompts.

Share what you make

Posts hold up to 280 characters and one image. Compose from the gallery, add a thought, publish. That is the whole loop.

Organise by collection

AI Art Studio, Fantasy Worlds, Lofi Prompts, Prompt Marketplace and Art Curation. Every image has a home.

Keep it human-sized

Five creators, 61 works, no backend. The site runs entirely in your browser and resets when you close the tab.

What we believe

Credit is part of the craft. Every artwork keeps the name of the person who prompted it. Prompts are visible, copyable and shareable.

Small rooms make better conversations. A timeline you can finish is a feature, not a bug. Follow a handful of people and actually see their work.

The tools should get out of the way. This site is three columns, four stylesheets and four JavaScript files. No build step, no tracking, no cleverness for its own sake.
