Help centre

How can we help?

Short answers about the feed, the gallery, prompts and this demo.

What is Orange Bird?

Orange Bird is a static demo social site for AI artists, built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. It recreates the flat, three-column feel of the early 2010s web.

Is my account real?

No. Login and signup are mocked in the browser. No passwords are stored or sent anywhere, and every session resets when you close the tab.

How do I publish a post?

Open Compose, write up to 280 characters, optionally pick an artwork from the gallery, then press Publish. Your post is stored in sessionStorage for this tab.

Where do the images come from?

All 61 JPGs ship with the project and live in assets/images/. They are grouped into five collections: AI Art, Fantasy, Lofi, Marketplace and Art Curation.

Can I use the prompts commercially?

The demo prompts are illustrative. Check the licence on the original artwork before using it commercially. Credit the artist when you do.

How does the marketplace work?

The Buy buttons are demonstration only. No payment is taken, and no order is created. Use the listings to explore pricing and presentation.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes. Put your images in assets/profile/, then follow the notes in README.md to add them to data.js and the profile media tab.

Why does my post disappear?

Posts, likes, bookmarks and profile edits use sessionStorage. Closing the browser tab clears them. This is intentional for a static demo.
